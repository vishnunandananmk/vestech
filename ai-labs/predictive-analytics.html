Our Forecasting Process

1. Data Discovery & Assessment: We analyze your historical data quality, identify predictive signals, and understand the business context and forecasting requirements.

2. Feature Engineering: Extract meaningful features including time-based patterns, external factors (holidays, weather, economic indicators), and domain-specific signals.

3. Model Development: Experiment with multiple approaches—from classical statistical methods to deep learning—selecting the best models for your specific forecasting challenge.

4. Uncertainty Quantification: Provide prediction intervals and confidence bounds, not just point forecasts. Enable risk-aware decision making.

5. Deployment & Monitoring: Implement automated forecasting pipelines with model monitoring, drift detection, and continuous improvement mechanisms.
